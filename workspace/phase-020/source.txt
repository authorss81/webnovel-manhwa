Sovereign Cao’s face went dark. He raised his hammer, a bright blue barrier of kinetic mana flaring around his body, pushing the green gas away from his chest.

Six spear-men stepped over their dying comrades, their long, steel-tipped shafts thrusting through the three-foot gap in the barrier toward Ren Qi’s chest.

Ren Qi stood his ground. He didn't dodge.

As the six steel spearheads rushed toward his chest, his shadow—Umbra—surged from the floor.

A thick, non-Euclidean wall of absolute blackness rose before him, absorbing the light from the Vanguard's halogen lamps. The steel spearheads plunged into the black pool, but they didn't hit Ren Qi's chest. They entered a spatial void, their momentum snuffed out instantly by the shadow-lineage primordial.

The spear-men pulled back, but their weapons were stuck. The black ink of the shadow had wrapped around the steel shafts, its toxic residue eating into the wood and metal with a faint, chemical smoke.

Ren Qi stepped forward, his boots heavy and steady on the wet concrete. He raised his Molecular Blade, the dark edge humming with its high-frequency vibration.

"Your lease," Ren Qi said, his voice flat and deadpan over the shrieks of the dying shield-bearers, "has been terminated."
