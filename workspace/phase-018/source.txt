# Chapter 18: The Subway Kingdom

The transition from the ancient stone gate in B3 to the ruined transit system below was marked by a sudden, violent drop in air pressure.

Ren Qi stepped through the shimmering, oily film of the spatial portal. The air on the other side did not belong to a sealed tomb; it belonged to a massive, drafty void that smelled of rusted iron, old electricity, and a heavy, organic sweetness that he immediately categorized as trouble.

Behind him, the spatial archway remained stable, a dark pool of liquid light anchored to the bedrock of Skyview Garden. He looked back once. The four indentured thugs were already setting up a defensive perimeter near the threshold, their hands trembling as they hauled concrete blocks to build a barricade. They did not want to go any deeper. That was fine. Ren Qi had no intention of bringing dead weight into an uncharted sector.

He turned his flashlight downward, the beam cutting through a thick, yellow-tinted mist that hung three feet off the ground.

He was standing on a concrete platform. The floor was tiled with non-slip ceramic squares, many of them cracked and pushed upward by thick, pale root structures. Above him, massive steel guide signs hung at a crooked angle, their plastic faces melted and warped by past heat. Through the grime, he could read the faded English and Chinese characters: Chegongmiao Interchange - Line 1, Line 7, Line 9, Line 11.

In the old days, this was one of the busiest transit hubs in Shenzhen. Millions of people had squeezed through these turnstiles, driven by the relentless rhythm of corporate deadlines and low-paying jobs. Now, it was silent. The only movement came from the slow, rhythmic pulsing of shelf-fungi that clung to the pillars like giant, glowing ears. They cast a pale, subterranean violet light across the tracks, painting the derailed metal carriages in the distance with the color of a fresh bruise.

Ren Qi took a deep breath. His Titan's Resilience skeleton, dense as industrial rebar, made the rusted tracks groan as he stepped down from the platform.

Every movement was heavy, but his three-hundred-pound frame felt perfectly balanced. His physical density gave him a center of gravity that made slips impossible. He pulled out his Molecular Blade, the dark, mono-molecular edge humming with a faint, high-frequency vibration that was barely audible over the dripping of water.

He opened his interface with a flick of his wrist.

[SYSTEM: You have entered 'The Subway Kingdom' (Dungeon Sector B-3).]

[Current Level: 6 (EXP: 420/1200)]

[Danger Rating: Moderate-High (Levels 8-15)]

[Regional Status: The hunt for 'Public Enemy Number One' is active. Dawn Vanguard forces are currently clearing the adjacent High-Tech Park Station. High player density detected within a 3-kilometer radius.]

Ren Qi looked at the regional status. A cold, analytical calculation ran through his mind. Sovereign Cao and the Dawn Vanguard were close. They were operating just a few stations away on Line 1, likely trying to secure the high-level resource nodes in the High-Tech Park sector.

Let them search. Down here, sixty meters beneath the asphalt, the concrete and steel of the subway tunnels acted as a natural shield against physical tracking and wireless detection. Unless they stumbled directly into his portal in B3, they would have no way of knowing he was sitting directly beneath their boots, harvesting the very foundation of their supply line.

He began to walk down the tracks of Line 11, heading west.

The silence of the tunnel was absolute, save for the occasional, wet drip-drop of condensation falling from the vaulted ceiling. The air here was cool, but it carried a strange, static charge that made the hair on his arms stand up. Every fifty meters, he passed an abandoned subway car. Some were completely crushed, their steel ribs exposed to the damp air like the skeletons of deep-sea whales. Others were pristine, their advertisement posters still intact, showcasing faces of long-forgotten celebrities smiling through layers of swily mold—the thick, oily residue that covered electronics when they rotted in high-humidity dungeon zones.

His calculation speed attribute, enhanced by his structural engineering background, picked up a micro-frequency change in the air.

Frequency: 14 Hertz. Interval: 0.8 seconds. Source: Fifty meters ahead, inside the dark interior of Car 4.

It was the sound of segmented legs dragging across rusted sheet metal.

Ren Qi extinguished his flashlight. He didn't need it. The fluorescent violet fungi growing along the cable trays provided enough illumination to outline the shapes of the tracks.

A shadow detached itself from the side of the derailed carriage. It was low to the ground, roughly the size of a modern sedan, its body protected by a thick, dark green carapace that looked like wet slate. Six jointed legs ended in sharp, yellow claws that clicked against the iron rails. Its head was small, dominated by a pair of massive, horizontal mandibles that ground together with a dry, wooden sound.

[Entity: Mycoal Carapace Beetle (Level 9)]

[Type: Insectoid/Mutant]

[Attributes: High physical defense, low speed, toxic blood.]

The beetle paused, its long antennae twitching as it picked up the scent of live flesh.

Ren Qi did not wait for it to make the first move. He calculated the distance—thirty-two meters. The slope of the track was slightly downward, giving him a gravity assist. He adjusted the grip on his Molecular Blade, keeping his elbow bent at a forty-five-degree angle to maximize the leverage of his high-density skeletal structure.

His boots shattered the dry ballast stones between the ties. Despite his three-hundred-pound weight, his movement was remarkably quiet, a sudden, heavy shadow that crossed the distance in less than two seconds.

The beetle sensed the vibration and reared back, its mandibles flaring open to expose a pair of pale, fleshy spinnerets.

Ren Qi didn't give it the chance to spray. He stepped to the left, his boot slamming into the concrete base of a signaling box, using the momentum to pivot behind the creature's blind spot. His Molecular Blade came down in a clean, vertical arc.

The blade didn't just cut; it separated the molecular bonds of the slate-like carapace. The left rear leg of the beetle was sheared off at the joint, releasing a thick, yellowish fluid that hissed as it hit the rusted tracks, eating into the metal with a faint, chemical smoke.

The creature shrieked, a high-pitched, metallic rasp that vibrated through the walls of the tunnel. It swung its heavy body around, trying to use its massive horn to impale him.

Ren Qi stood his ground. He didn't dodge.

As the horn rushed toward his chest, he raised his left hand, his palm flat. His skin, reinforced by the Titan's Resilience serum, was as tough as vulcanized rubber.

The horn collided with his palm. The impact was massive, a force that would have shattered the collarbone of any ordinary Level 6 player. But Ren Qi's body simply absorbed the kinetic energy, transferring the shock downward through his legs and into the concrete floor, cracking the grout beneath his boots.

He didn't budge an inch.

With his right hand, he drove the Molecular Blade upward, straight through the soft, pale flesh beneath the beetle's lower jaw. The blade slid in up to the hilt, puncturing the central nerve cluster.

The creature stiffened. The light in its compound eyes went dark, and its massive body collapsed onto the ballast with a heavy, wet thud.

[SYSTEM: You have slain a Mycoal Carapace Beetle (Level 9).]

[Obtained: 1x Virulent Core (Rank F).]

Ren Qi pulled his blade free, wiping the corrosive yellow blood onto a patch of dry moss. He reached into the creature's split chest cavity and extracted the core. Unlike the standard grey cores he had harvested from the ghouls in Skyview Garden, this one was a deep, translucent green, pulsing with a faint, internal light.

He weighed it in his hand.

[Item: Virulent Core (Rank F)]

[Properties: High chemical energy, toxic residue. Can be used as fuel or raw material for physical transmutations.]

"Good," Ren Qi murmured. "A new chemical base."

He continued down the tunnel, his eyes scanning the sides. He was looking for a specific resource. The database he had purchased from the broker Fox had mentioned that the deep sections of the Chegongmiao Interchange were home to a unique flora—the Grave-Spore Fungus.

Ten minutes later, he found it.

The tunnel opened into a massive cavern where the ceiling had collapsed long ago, creating a subterranean dome. Here, the air was warm and thick with a dense, green fog that hung like a curtain. Growing from the piles of concrete rubble were hundreds of giant, umbrella-shaped mushrooms. Their caps were a pale, sickly white, covered in dark green spots that dripped a thick, luminous mucus onto the ground.

The ground was covered in a carpet of bones—mostly rats, ghouls, and a few larger, skeletal remains that looked suspiciously human.

This was the nesting ground of the Stalker-Widow.

Ren Qi stopped at the edge of the fungal forest. He could feel the toxicity of the air even through his skin. Every breath tasted like copper and vinegar. His lungs felt tight, but his high physical constitution kept the damage at bay for now.

He stepped onto the concrete rubble.

A dried bone cracked under his boot.

The response was immediate. From the center of the fungal grove, a massive shape descended from the ceiling, hanging from a thick, greasy strand of grey web.

It was an arachnid, but its body was deformed by the system's mystery integration. Its abdomen was the size of a water tank, shaped like a human skull fused with rotting, green vegetation. Its legs were long, thin, and tipped with needle-like claws that looked like black glass.

[Entity: Stalker-Widow (Level 13 - Elite)]

[Type: Insectoid/Necrotic]

[Attributes: Lethal toxic strike, high mobility, invisible web lines.]

The creature did not make a sound. It simply dropped.

Ren Qi's calculation speed attribute screamed. He threw his body to the right, but the space was too narrow. A thick, grey line of web shot from the creature's spinnerets, catching his left shoulder.

The web was not sticky; it was acidic. The moment it touched the Cuirass of the Iron Eater, the rusted iron scales began to hiss, the metal dissolving into a red, bubbling paste. The heat penetrated his clothes, burning his skin.

Before he could recover his balance, the Stalker-Widow closed the distance. Its movement was a blur of black glass legs.

One of its forelegs thrust forward like a spear.

Ren Qi raised his blade to block, but the speed of the Level 13 Elite was far higher than the slow carapace beetles he had fought before. The glass-like tip of the leg bypassed his guard, punching straight through the leather strap of his chest armor and burying itself three inches deep into his right collarbone.

The pain was not sharp; it was cold.

An icy fluid flooded his chest, spreading through his veins like liquid nitrogen. His vision instantly began to blur, the violet light of the tunnel turning into a series of jagged, dark lines.

[SYSTEM WARNING: You have been afflicted by 'Stalker-Widow Necro-Toxin' (High-Grade).]

[Health is decaying at 5% per second.]

[Nerve decay initiated. Motor function loss in 24 seconds. Total organ failure in 40 seconds.]

Ren Qi stumbled back, his right arm going completely numb. The Molecular Blade slipped from his fingers, clattering against the concrete.

The Stalker-Widow hissed, its horizontal mandibles grinding together as it prepared to deliver the final, meat-liquefying bite. It crept closer, its skull-like abdomen pulsing with a dark, green light.

His heart was pumping the poison directly toward his brain. He could feel the coldness reaching his neck. His Titan's Resilience was fighting it, his dense bone marrow producing white blood cells at a desperate rate, but it was a losing battle against a Level 13 Elite toxin.

He had twenty seconds to live.

Ren Qi did not look at the approaching spider. He did not look at his dying health bar.

He looked at his own veins. Through his flannel shirt, the blood vessels in his neck and chest were standing out, black and swollen, tracing a dark, geometric pattern across his skin.

The poison was in his blood. His blood was his property.

His property was subject to exchange.

"System," Ren Qi rasped, his voice barely a whisper through his swollen throat.

[Inequivalent Exchange.]

He placed his left hand over his heart, his fingers digging into his flesh until he could feel the cold, thick pulse of the contaminated blood beneath his skin.

"I don't want an antidote," Ren Qi muttered. His eyes, though clouded by the poison, were steady, filled with a cold, terrifying clarity. "An antidote is a trade. It leaves me where I started. I want the poison. I want the concept of this lethality to become a part of my structural design."

[Target: Stalker-Widow Necro-Toxin (Active in Cardiovascular System).]

[Input: Toxin + 220 Mana + Physical Trauma.]

[Concept: Destruction -> Integration.]

[SYSTEM WARNING: This exchange requires the permanent alteration of your biological profile. Your body density and cellular structure will adapt to tolerate extreme virulence, but you will permanently lose 2 points of basic Charisma. Current Charisma: 3. Projected Charisma: 1. Do you wish to proceed?]

"Proceed," Ren Qi said.

The world went white.

It was not the sound of an explosion, but the sound of his own blood cells rupturing and reforming. The SSS-Rank talent did not simply neutralize the toxin; it rewrote the molecular formula of his blood, turning his veins into a sterile, highly adaptive culture medium.

The black lines under his skin began to fade, but they did not disappear. They turned a faint, iridescent green, settling deep into his capillaries.

[Exchange Successful.]

[Input: High-Grade Necro-Toxin + 220 Mana + 2 Charisma.]

[Output: Passive Skill - Venom Affinity (Rank C), 1x Refined Necro-Extract (Rank D).]

The coldness in his chest vanished, replaced by a sudden, hot wave of vitality. His motor function snapped back into place. His vision cleared, the jagged dark lines resolving into a high-definition view of the cavern.

The Stalker-Widow was only three feet away, its mandibles open, its head lowered to strike.

Ren Qi did not grab his blade.

He reached out with both hands, his fingers wrapping around the creature's front legs. His grip was like vice-grips, his dense muscles delivering a crushing force that cracked the glass-like shell of the arachnid.

The spider thrashed, trying to pull back, but Ren Qi's three-hundred-pound body was anchored to the ground like a concrete pillar.

"You're a good resource," Ren Qi said.

He twisted his hands.

The Stalker-Widow's legs snapped. He threw the giant insect onto its back, his boot coming down with the force of a hydraulic press straight into the center of its skull-like abdomen.

The green light inside the shell flickered, then exploded into a shower of dark, viscous fluid and pale green spores.

[SYSTEM: You have slain 'Stalker-Widow' (Level 13 - Elite).]

[Obtained: 1x Elite Necro-Core (Rank E), 3x Stalker-Widow Silk (Rank E).]

Ren Qi stood in the rain of green fluid, his clothes soaked, his skin shining with a faint, iridescent sheen. He didn't feel sick. In fact, as the toxic spores landed on his skin, they were absorbed through his pores, turning into a cool sensation that slowly replenished his drained mana.

He checked his status.

[Passive Skill: Venom Affinity (Rank C)]

[Description: The user's body has been structurally adapted to tolerate and metabolize biological toxins. Immunity to low and mid-grade poisons. High-grade poisons are converted into mana restoration (1% of max mana per second of exposure).]

He looked at the forest of Grave-Spore Fungi around him.

The green fog that hung over the cavern was no longer a hazard. It was a fuel station. As long as he stood in this room, his mana would recover at a rate that bypassed the natural limits of his low level.

A dry, calculated smile touched his lips.

He looked at the pale green mushrooms. He looked at the shattered remains of the Stalker-Widow.

Sovereign Cao and the Dawn Vanguard were clearing the High-Tech Park Station. The database had mentioned that the High-Tech Park was infested with Green-Spore Creepers—monsters that used the exact same necro-toxin as the Stalker-Widow. The Vanguard was losing men every day to the poison, and their healers were running out of mana trying to purge the rot.

They needed a solution.

Ren Qi reached into his pocket and pulled out a small glass vial of clean water from his Skyview Garden inventory. He gathered a handful of the white, green-spotted caps of the Grave-Spore Fungi and placed them into the water.

"System," he murmured.

[Inequivalent Exchange.]

[Input: Grave-Spore Fungus + Clean Water + 50 Mana.]

[Concept: Poison -> Neutralization.]

The water in the vial began to boil. The green spots on the mushroom caps dissolved, turning the liquid into a clear, golden syrup that smelled faintly of pine needles.

[Exchange Successful.]

[Output: Formula - Grave-Spore Neutralizer (Rank D).]

[Description: Instantly cures all mid-grade fungal and insectoid poisons. Grants 1 hour of total toxin immunity to the user.]

Ren Qi held the golden vial up to the light.

The cost to produce this was practically zero. The water was free from his basement pumps. The mushrooms were growing in wild abundance right here in his backyard. The only real cost was 50 points of his mana—which recovered instantly as long as he stood in the toxic fog of this cavern.

He could produce hundreds of these vials a day.

He opened his system market interface, navigating to the regional trade board of District 9.

The prices for basic antidotes were sky-high. The automated Abyssal Kiosks sold standard Rank E antidotes for 30 Rank E Cores each, and their stock was limited to five vials per day per district. On the black market, desperate guilds were paying up to 45 cores for a single dose.

Ren Qi listed his prototype vial on the forum under an anonymous seller tag: The Landlord.

[Item: Grave-Spore Neutralizer (Rank D)]

[Price: 15 Rank E Cores.]

[Quantity: 1 (Sample).]

Within three seconds, the listing disappeared.

A notification chimed in his ear.

[SYSTEM: Transaction complete. You have received 15 Rank E Cores (Stored in System Escrow).]

Ren Qi's eyes narrowed.

15 cores for a few weeds and fifty points of mana that he had already recovered. The margin was astronomical.

But this was only the beginning.

He didn't want to just sell them a cure. He wanted to control their progression.

If he sold the antidotes too cheaply, the Dawn Vanguard would clear the High-Tech Park too quickly, grow too strong, and eventually come back to hunt him down with their high-level squads.

But if he sold them just enough to survive, they would keep fighting, keep spending their resources, and keep funneling their hard-earned cores directly into his pocket. He would be taxing their survival. Every step they took into the High-Tech Park would be funded by him, and every core they harvested from the monsters there would eventually find its way back down to the subway station beneath their feet.

He looked around the cavern.

This wasn't a dungeon to be cleared and abandoned.

He walked back toward the platform, his boots heavy and steady on the concrete. He needed to get the four thugs down here. They had tools, they had muscles, and they had a binding contract that made them perfectly obedient.

They were going to clear the rubble. They were going to build concrete cultivation beds.

They were going to farm the poison that would run the economy of District 9.

Ren Qi reached the spatial portal at the platform of Line 11. He looked back at the dark, violet-lit tunnels stretching into the subterranean depths of the city.

The surface world was fighting for survival, wasting their lives in bloody, chaotic raids.

Ren Qi preferred a more structured approach.

He stepped through the portal, back into the warm, humming basement of Skyview Garden, his pockets heavy with the green cores of the Subway Kingdom.

"Lin Xia," his low, bureaucratic voice echoed through the concrete stairs as he emerged. "Get the empty bottles from the recycling room. We have a new product line to package."
