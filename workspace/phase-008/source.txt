Chapter 8: The Butcher of Section C

Ren Qi stood on the landing of the 14th floor. The air here was different.

Up on the 24th, the air was thin, smelling of ozone and the cold sterilization of his Purification Spirit. Down in the lobby, it was a frozen tomb. But here, in the mid-section—what the building directory listed as the "Executive Suites"—the air was thick enough to chew.

It smelled of copper. It smelled of meat left out in the sun.

Ren adjusted the grip on his Molecular Blade. His palm was sweating inside the leather glove. The Purification Spirit water kept his muscles from seizing, but it couldn't scrub the adrenaline from his blood.

He checked the Master Key hanging from his belt. It was heavy, reassuring. A golden promise that he could lock the door on anything he couldn't kill.

But he couldn't lock the 14th floor. Not yet. He needed to clear it.

The hallway stretched out before him, carpeted in a plush, dark red that hid stains too well. The emergency lights flickered with a dying, epileptic strobe.

A heavy, wet sound. Like a side of beef dropped on concrete.

It came from the end of the corridor. Unit 1408.

Ren moved. He didn't creep; creeping implies hesitation. He moved with the fluid, rolling gait of a man who knows that noise is a variable to be managed, not eliminated. He hugged the wall.

He raised the Specter-Sight Scope.

The hallway washed out in grayscale. He scanned the doors. 1402. 1404.

At the far end, the wall of Unit 1408 was gone. Blown outward.

Standing in the debris was a mountain.

Ren zoomed in. His stomach tightened, a hard knot of biological rejection.

It wasn't a man. It wasn't a Ghoul.

The creature stood eight feet tall. Its skin was a patchwork quilt of human flesh—different tones, different textures, stitched together with thick, black industrial wire. The stitches pulled tight, burying into the fat. One arm was massive, the bicep swollen with implanted muscle, ending not in a hand, but in a heavy, rusted meat cleaver fused directly to the radius bone. The other arm was withered, small, seemingly vestigial, clutching a hook on a chain.

It wore a leather apron stained so black it shone like oil.

Above the apron, the face was a ruin. No eyes. Just a mouth sewn shut, and a nose that had been cut off, leaving a gaping, snorting hole.

[Target: The Flesh-Stitched Butcher (Boss - Elite)]

[Attributes: Monstrous Strength. Pain Immunity. Necrotic Aura.]

[Warning: Danger Level - Fatal.]

Ren lowered the scope.

Math. It was always just math. The Gatekeeper had been Level 15, but it was slow, blind, and Ren had the high ground and a turret. This thing was in a narrow hallway. It had reach. It had mass.

If that cleaver hit him, Mental Fortress wouldn't matter. He would be bisected.

The creature turned. It didn't have eyes, but it snapped its head toward Ren with terrifying precision. The hole in its face flared, inhaling the scent of fresh blood and sweat.

It let out a sound. A wet, tearing noise from the sewn-shut mouth.

It didn't shamble. It charged like a locomotive, the floorboards groaning under the impact of its boots.

Ren didn't fight. He turned and ran.

He sprinted back toward the stairwell.

The vibrations traveled up his legs. It was fast. Too fast for something that size.

Ren reached the stairwell door. He slammed his shoulder into the bar.

He fumbled for the Master Key.

The hook on the chain smashed into the doorframe inches from his head, embedding itself in the steel jamb. Sparks showered Ren’s hair.

Ren ducked, rolling sideways. He abandoned the door. He couldn't stop to unlock it. The animation time—the second it took to utter the command—would be the second he died.

He scrambled back into the hallway.

He was trapped on the 14th floor. A straight corridor, fifty meters long, ten meters wide.

The Butcher retracted the chain with a ratchet-like grinding noise. It stepped closer, filling the width of the hall. It raised the cleaver arm.

Ren backed up. His boot hit something.

A shoe rack. Outside Unit 1402.

A tenant had left their muddy hiking boots and a pair of high heels outside.

Ren looked at the Butcher. Twenty meters away. Gathering speed for a charge.

Ren looked at the boots.

"Resources," Ren hissed.

He grabbed a high-heeled shoe. Red lacquer. Cheap.

[Target: High Heel Shoe (Damaged)]

[Exchange Available.]

Ren squeezed the heel.

The shoe dissolved. The red lacquer liquefied, expanding, turning viscous and pungent. The leather melted into a dark, oily sludge.

[Exchange Successful.]

[You have obtained: Vial of the Slick (Rank D)]

[Attributes: Friction coefficient reduced to zero. Applies a coat of everlasting grease.]

Ren didn't hesitate. He threw the vial.

It shattered on the carpet five meters in front of the charging Butcher.

The liquid exploded outward, soaking into the rug. The plush red fabric turned black and glistening.

The Butcher hit the patch.

Physics took over. The creature’s forward momentum was massive. Its lead foot hit the grease and slid forward as if on ice. The traction vanished.

The Butcher’s legs flew out from under it.

It slammed onto its back, the impact shaking dust from the ceiling tiles. The cleaver gouged a deep furrow in the wall as it flailed.

It roared—a muffled scream behind the stitches.

Ren didn't watch. He ran.

He needed distance. He needed a kill box.

He sprinted past Unit 1405. An umbrella stand sat by the door, holding a long, black umbrella with a curved handle.

The Butcher was scrambling up, its oily apron slick with grease, slipping and thrashing. It was angry now.

Ren held the umbrella.

[Target: Nylon Umbrella]

[Exchange Available.]

The metal ribs of the umbrella snapped and twisted. The black nylon hardened, sharpening into jagged, iron thorns. The handle elongated into a heavy, spiked base.

[Exchange Successful.]

[You have obtained: The Iron Maiden’s Rib (Rank D)]

[Attributes: Deployable blockade. Spikes inflict 'Bleed' and 'Tetanus' status.]

Ren dropped the device in the center of the hallway. It sprang open with a violent clack, expanding into a waist-high barricade of rusted iron spikes.

He wasn't trying to escape anymore. He was leading it.

He checked the floor.

He was over the structural seam of the building. The expansion joint between the east and west wings.

He pulled the Molecular Blade.

He activated Eye of the Void.

The headache spiked, a white-hot needle behind his eyes, but the world stripped down to its wireframe bones.

He saw the floor joists beneath the carpet. Steel beams, rusted but strong.

He saw the stress loads.

Ren dropped to one knee. He jammed the Molecular Blade into the floor.

The blade sank through the carpet, through the subfloor, and bit into the steel beam.

Ren dragged the blade in a wide arc. He wasn't fighting an enemy; he was editing the architecture.

He sliced a four-meter square section of the floor, leaving only a few inches of steel connecting it at the corners.

The floor groaned. It was a trapdoor waiting for a trigger.

Ren stood up. He stood on the far side of his cut, putting the trap between him and the monster.

The Butcher was back on its feet.

It saw the spike barricade. It didn't care. It didn't try to climb over.

The creature didn't feel pain. It barreled into the Iron Maiden’s Rib. The rusted spikes tore into its patchwork legs, ripping stitches, spilling black ichor. The Butcher didn't slow down. It trampled the iron trap flat, bending the metal with sheer mass.

It raised the cleaver.

Ren stood still. He needed it to commit. He needed the momentum.

If the Butcher stopped to look at the floor, the plan failed. If it walked slowly, the remaining steel tabs might hold its weight for a second—long enough for it to jump.

He needed it to run blindly.

Ren reached into his pocket. He pulled out the Prometheus Zippo.

Not enough. Fire wouldn't stop it.

He needed a flash. A distraction.

He looked around. Nothing. The hallway was bare here.

His pocket. The phone he had taken the picture of Lin Xia’s screen with.

Ren pulled it out. Battery at 11%.

He turned on the flashlight. Max brightness.

[Target: Smartphone (Low Battery)]

[Exchange Available.]

The Butcher was ten meters away. The smell of rot washed over Ren like a wave.

"Exchange," Ren whispered.

The phone grew hot in his hand. The glass screen cracked, glowing with a blinding, magnesium-white light. The battery acid boiled, converting electrical potential into pure luminescence.

[Exchange Successful.]

[You have obtained: Star-Shell Flare (Consumable)]

Ren shielded his eyes and crushed the device in his hand.

It was like a supernova in a closet.

The hallway vanished into a world of pure, erasing white. Shadows were obliterated.

The Butcher shrieked. It had no eyes, but the necrotic nerves in its face were sensitive to light. The sudden overload fried its sensory perception.

It didn't stop running. It couldn't. It was a freight train with the brakes cut.

It charged forward, blindly swinging the cleaver.

The Butcher’s heavy boot hit the carpet.

Right in the center of the square Ren had cut.

The sound of steel shearing was louder than the monster’s roar.

The floor simply vanished beneath it.

Gravity, the oldest god in the building, claimed its tithe.

The Butcher pitched forward. Its momentum carried it face-first into the edge of the solid floor where Ren stood. Its chin—or where its chin should have been—slammed into the concrete lip.

It fell through the hole, tumbling into the darkness of the 13th floor below.

Ren heard it land. A heavy, bone-breaking impact amidst the furniture of the apartment below.

He walked to the edge of the hole.

Through the Eye of the Void, he saw the creature below. It was stunned. Its health bar had dropped by 30%. The fall had broken its legs.

But it was healing. The black wires were writhing, knitting the bones back together.

"Finish it," Ren said.

He dropped through the hole in the floor.

As he fell, he oriented himself. He held the Molecular Blade with both hands, pointing downward.

The Butcher lay on its back in the ruins of a crushed dining table. It sensed movement above. It tried to raise the cleaver.

Ren fell like a spear.

He drove the Molecular Blade into the center of the creature’s chest, right where the stitches met in a gnarly, keloid star.

The blade didn't stop. It passed through the sternum, through the corrupted heart, and pinned the monster to the floor of the 13th level.

The vibration of the blade liquefied the organs instantly.

The Butcher convulsed. The cleaver arm spasmed, shaving a layer of paint off the wall inches from Ren’s ear.

Then, the black wires went slack. The patchwork flesh turned gray.

[Experience Gained: 2,500]

[Ren Qi: Level 2 -> Level 3]

Ren stayed crouched on the corpse for a moment, breathing hard. His lungs burned. The Purification Spirit was working overtime to clear the toxins of the creature's proximity from his blood.

He ripped the blade free. A fountain of black sludge followed it.

He stood up, wiping the slime from his face.

The room on the 13th floor was quiet.

He looked at the corpse. It was beginning to dissolve, the unstable magic that held it together failing.

Something glinted in the mess of melting flesh.

He pulled out two items.

The first was a spool of the black wire.

[Item: Necro-Stitch Thread (Rare)]

[Description: Unbreakable binding material. Can be used to repair organic tissue or reinforce structural weaknesses.]

Ren pocketed it. Useful.

The second item was heavier. It was the cleaver. It had detached from the creature’s arm as it melted.

It was rusted, nasty, and heavy as a tombstone.

[Item: The Butcher’s Mercy (Rank C)]

[Attributes: Heavy Cleaver. Inflicts 'Deep Wound'. Damage increases based on the user's missing health.]

Ren hefted it. It was too unbalanced for him. He preferred the precision of the Molecular Blade.

But it was mass. It was material.

[Exchange Available.]

Ren looked at the cleaver.

He thought about what he needed. He didn't need a sword. He had one.

He needed armor. He was wearing jeans and a jacket. If that thing had touched him, he would be dead.

The rusted metal of the cleaver groaned. It liquefied, flowing over Ren’s hand, up his arm. It didn't burn; it felt cold and heavy.

It settled over his chest.

The metal hardened, turning into a fitted vest of dark, interlocking scales. It was light, silent, but Ren could feel the density of it.

[Exchange Successful.]

[You have obtained: Cuirass of the Iron Eater (Rank B)]

[Attributes: Physical damage reduction +30%. Absorbs kinetic impact and stores it as a localized charge for the next melee strike.]

Ren tapped the chest piece. It made a dull, solid sound.

He looked up at the hole in the ceiling. The 14th floor.

He looked around the 13th floor.

"Twenty-three floors to go," Ren muttered.

He checked his energy. The Level Up had restored his stamina. The fatigue was gone, replaced by a fresh, thrumming power. His senses felt sharper. The Eye of the Void didn't hurt as much when he blinks.

He walked to the door of Unit 1308. He unlocked it.

The hallway of the 13th floor was empty.
